# Memory Seed market size

## Purpose and current conclusion

This dossier defines the addressable market for Memory Seed without presenting the whole agentic-AI,
knowledge-management, or developer-tools market as obtainable revenue.

Current working view:

| Market boundary | Annual revenue opportunity | Confidence |
|---|---:|---|
| Narrow current market: agent and project-memory infrastructure | **$300M-$800M** | Low-medium |
| Initial serviceable market: technical teams and agent builders | **$1.4B-$4.5B** | Medium |
| Theoretical global developer TAM at $19/month | **$11.0B** | High arithmetic; low attainability |
| Five-year obtainable revenue for Memory Seed | **$15M-$55M ARR** | Low; planning hypothesis |

The recommended external framing is an emerging **decision and institutional memory infrastructure**
category within agentic AI, initially sold through developer and engineering-team use cases.

## Memory Seed estimates and methodology

### Narrow current category

There is no dependable published market total for AI-agent memory alone. We model memory and context
infrastructure as **5%-15%** of 2026 enterprise agentic-AI spending:

```text
$5.3B x 5%  = $265M
$5.3B x 15% = $795M
Rounded planning range = $300M-$800M
```

The allocation is an internal assumption. It needs validation through vendor revenue, funding,
customer-spend, and procurement evidence.

### Developer-led serviceable market

At the current competitor median entry price of **$19/month**:

```text
19.9M cloud-native developers x $19 x 12 = $4.537B
```

The lower bound assumes roughly 30% of that population is plausibly reachable and willing to pay:

```text
$4.537B x 30% = $1.361B, rounded to $1.4B
```

This is a serviceable-market model, not a forecast of actual category revenue.

### Theoretical developer TAM

```text
48.4M developers x $19 x 12 = $11.035B, rounded to $11.0B
```

Adoption sensitivity:

| Paid adoption | Customers | Annual revenue pool |
|---:|---:|---:|
| 5% | 2.42M | $551.8M |
| 10% | 4.84M | $1.104B |
| 20% | 9.68M | $2.207B |
| 40% | 19.36M | $4.414B |

### Obtainable market

The **$15M-$55M ARR** five-year range is an internal ambition envelope, not a bottom-up forecast. It
must be replaced with a cohort model once Memory Seed has observed conversion, retention, expansion,
and enterprise contract data.

## Category boundaries

Memory Seed overlaps three markets:

1. Agent memory and context infrastructure: the closest narrow market.
2. Developer and engineering intelligence: the initial distribution and budget wedge.
3. Enterprise knowledge and decision infrastructure: a long-term adjacency, not current TAM.

Avoid using broad enterprise search, knowledge management, or total AI economic impact as the primary
TAM. They contain spending Memory Seed cannot credibly serve today.

## Unresolved validation questions

- What share of production-agent infrastructure spend is actually allocated to memory and context?
- How many cloud-native developers are addressable buyers rather than users covered by an employer?
- Which budget owner pays first: individual developer, engineering leader, platform team, or AI team?
- Does the initial paid value come from hosted memory, governance, collaboration, or Memory Trace?
- What conversion and retention rates emerge from the first 100 active projects?
