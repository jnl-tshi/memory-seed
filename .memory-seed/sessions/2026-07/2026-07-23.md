### Decision

- D: The deliverable is a `docs/2_Todo` proposal for JNL to react to, not an `agent-rules.md` edit. It
  proposes two variable groups - session-stable (checked at first message: checkout_posture,
  integration_mode, a new merge_trigger, governing_persona, version_state) and intent-dependent (set from
  the task: write_intent, risk_tier, orchestration_level, worktree_decision, skills_to_load) - in a
  dependency-ordered sequence, and it carries six open questions rather than settled answers.
- R: JNL chose "let me design it, then react" over a fixed schema or a prose checklist, and chose a
  routine in agent-rules.md over a hook. A proposal is the artifact that honours both: it commits the
  design to paper where JNL can amend it, and it changes nothing until accepted. Writing it as an
  agent-rules.md edit would have pre-empted the review JNL explicitly asked for, and agent-rules.md is a
  locked control-plane file besides.
- A: The framing insight the proposal turns on is that the FIRST MESSAGE is where intent arrives -
  every existing orientation surface (SessionStart hook, situate) fires before the user has said
  anything, so it can only establish facts, not intent-dependent decisions. That single seam is what
  splits the schema and decides the mechanism: judgment variables cannot live in a hook at all, which is
  the concrete reason JNL's routing choice is not just a preference. I also grounded it in this session
  rather than in the abstract: the worktree decision and the merge-hold were BOTH made ad hoc here, and
  both are exactly the variables the gate would force - which is the proposal's own acceptance test.
- A: merge_trigger is argued as a genuine variable, not a restatement of integration_mode. It is an
  orthogonal axis (how-you-integrate vs when/who-triggers-it) and it is upstream of session-logging:
  manual is what lets multiple branches accumulate their own on-branch entries, which is the "log on
  branch, not main" discipline already in durable memory. So it is not a convenience toggle. The default
  is proposed (manual under local-merge) but left as open question 1 rather than asserted.
- A: Explicitly kept OUT of scope, and said so in the proposal: no persistent variable store (the
  Constitution's derived-projection invariant would then have to account for it), no new hook as source
  of truth, no change to integration_mode, no automating the merge decision away. The variables are
  established-and-acted-on, not persisted.
