### Records

#### D1 - Decision: Evidence packs carry the governing Constitution clauses; the whole document is only the fallback
- D: A v2 pack selects anchored clauses through one cascade in `constitution_projection.py`. Mandated clauses come from explicit anchors, the bindings of ADRs the spec names, and single-clause `S:` anchors. Ranked clauses are then chosen by one combined score: bindings of related ADRs, a topic-to-clause map, and BM25F keywords. At most 8 are kept, within 4,000 tokens. The whole document is included only when nothing matches. Task Packets feed the same cascade from `constitution_refs`, their ranking terms and the tier target.
  - Scope: resolver v2 packs and Task Packet projection; v1 specs unchanged.
  - Disposition: implemented
- R: The whole 11.7k-token Constitution took over half of a 20k pack, crowding out decisions and ADRs. JNL: the relevant section should be pulled, with whole as the fallback.
  - The topic map comes from ADR topics × governing/supporting bindings, so it needs no new store. The five clauses no ADR binds get authored `constitution-topics` markers (v2.5 evolution).
  - A clause-count limit was added after a probe showed short clauses filled the token cap with 26 of 30 clauses.
- A: Authored tags on all 30 clauses (JNL chose derived plus authored gaps). Keeping the old bag-of-words ranking (it matched on any shared word).
- F: memory_seed/constitution_projection.py, memory_seed/retrieval.py, memory_seed/retrieval_spec.py, memory_seed/task_packet.py, docs/CONSTITUTION.md

#### D2 - Decision: Design Discovery looks up its authority and turns per-item verdicts into write-time links
- D: `memory-seed discovery evidence` resolves the new `design-discovery` profile for an area. Its input is topics, keywords, pins and an optional query, whose `memory_search` hits (with `preferred_keywords`) become required pins. It returns related decisions, the owning ADRs and the governing clauses. `discovery assess` takes one verdict per evidence item (`replaces`, `refines`, `builds-on`, `related`, `no-edge`, `conflicts`, `governed-by`) and returns:
  - the authority table;
  - the `decisions[].links` envelope;
  - the `consulted` entry ids;
  - the ADR outcomes the append will require;
  - conflicts to ask about.
  - Scope: design_discovery.md step 2, Record item 3, step 6; session_logging.md step 8; history_retrieval.md; CLI and MCP.
  - Disposition: implemented
- R: Planning is when an agent has just read the ancestors, so the links are cheapest and most accurate then.
