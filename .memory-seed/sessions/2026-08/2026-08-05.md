  Zero fabrications and 8/8 on never-stored traps; every miss was an over-abstention traced to

### Decisions

#### D1 - Honour the kill condition and locate the fix in routing, not honesty

- D: Do not submit to Verging Labs v0.2. The recorded fix is (1) route durable non-decision facts
  to index.md at capture time - the home memory_consolidation.md already assigns them - and
  (2) let retrieval trust project files and superseding-entry context it already holds; re-run the
  dry-run and submit only if it clears 75.1.

  cheap; a published 71.0 is a permanent number. The diagnosis is unusually clean: all nine misses
  are "not recorded" answers, never wrong assertions, splitting into capture loss (the kickoff
  session recorded zero entries, so roster/cadence/budget/codename died at storage - E5's capture
  finding replayed on diary-style sessions with no code change) and under-confidence (Sofia,
  the original CI budget, and the Granite codename were all on disk and denied). Meanwhile both
  pre-registered strengths held: false-memory 8/8 with zero fabrications - the axis the published
  winner failed on - and updated-facts/thread-growth strong. Loosening the honesty posture to
  score higher would trade away the one property the field's own commentary says matters most for
  acting agents; routing and confidence are the real defects.
- A: Submitting anyway on the strength of the perfect false-memory column was declined - the
  blended number is what gets published. Widening the answer-key tolerance was declined as
  score-shopping. Re-running immediately tonight was declined: the routing fix is a control-plane
  change that deserves its own considered slice, not a 2am patch.
