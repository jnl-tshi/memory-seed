### Summary

- Made retrieval decision-level: `granularity="decision"` serves one result per `#### Dn` DRAFT
  block, keyed `mse_x:dN`, carrying the whole block plus that decision's own topics and lifecycle
  edges - and made it the memory_search default. Answer visibility at rank 1 went 2/8 to 5/8 on the
  dry-run store.

### Decisions

#### D1 - Serve retrieval at decision granularity with canonical mse_x:dN identity

- D: New `granularity="decision"` mode, default for `memory_search` and `rank_session_memory`:
  one chunk per `#### Dn - ...` heading with `chunk_id = mse_<entry>:dN`, decision-scoped topics
  (`operations:d1` resolves to that decision only; bare topics apply to all) and decision-scoped
  lifecycle edges. Entries without decision headings keep the whole-entry unit.
- R: Every other subsystem - topics, lifecycle edges, ADRs, Trace's D1/D2 selector - already speaks
  decision identity; retrieval was the only one still working in entries, which is why an entry
  holding three unrelated decisions was scored and served as one blob. Diagnosis from the E9
  dry-run: search recall was 100% (7 of 7 entries returned on a 7-entry store) and the agent still
  answered "not recorded", because 59% of each 280-char excerpt was identical YAML frontmatter and
  the facts sat in bodies the excerpt never reached. Decision chunks make the served unit the thing
  the agent must reason about, and they largely dissolve the length bias too - entry chunks span
  1,945-11,855 chars, decision chunks 1,125 mean.
- A: Character-budget excerpt tuning was JNL's first target and was rejected as treating the
  symptom. Serving whole entries was priced and rejected (18.6k tokens for 8 results against 2.3k
  for decisions). Keeping entry as the default and making decision opt-in was rejected: an opt-in
  default nobody sets is the state that produced the misses.
- F: `memory_seed/semantic_cache.py` (`_find_decision_ranges`, `_decision_topics`, decision branch),
  `memory_seed/retrieval.py`, `memory_seed/mcp_server.py`, `tests/test_decision_retrieval.py`.
- T: 7 new tests + full suite 929 passed. Answer-visible@1 on the dry-run store: entry 2/8,
  decision 5/8.

#### D2 - Serve the whole DRAFT block, not a preview

- D: A decision result's `excerpt` is the complete block up to `DECISION_TEXT_LIMIT = 2500` chars,
  newlines preserved, with an explicit truncation marker naming `memory_get_chunk` past the cap.
  Entry and section results keep the 280-char preview.
- R: JNL's instruction - give the agent the whole relevant DRAFT section, not the D and R lines.
  The block is the unit of reasoning, and a preview of a decision is exactly the failure the
  dry-run measured. Priced first: 2,250 tokens for a typical 8-result search and 3,616 at p90,
  against 560 today and 18,648 for whole entries; 2,500 chars covers 97.6% of recorded decisions in
  this repo, so truncation is the rare case and it announces itself rather than silently cutting a
  rationale in half.
- A: Extracting only D/R lines was my proposal and JNL corrected it - A/F/T carry the rejected
  alternatives and the validation, which is the part that stops a decision being re-litigated.
- F: `memory_seed/retrieval.py` (`_decision_body`, both result builders).
- T: `test_search_serves_whole_draft_block_not_a_preview` pins the full rationale present and no
  ellipsis; verified live that the Sofia decision now serves its complete D/R where the old excerpt
  showed only YAML.

#### D3 - Classify relevance in the tool so abstention is tool-reported

- D: `memory_search` attaches `relevance` (strong/weak/none) per result, `no_match_above_threshold`
  to the payload, and `relevance_rule` stating the rule in words. Strong = clears an absolute floor
  AND holds >=55% of the top score.
- R: The agent was being handed raw floats - 51.6 for an irrelevant hit, 36.5 for the one holding
  the answer - and asked to infer existence from them. That inference is what produced nine
  "not recorded" answers over facts that were on disk. Moving the classification into the tool
  makes "not recorded" a fact the tool reports rather than a judgement the model guesses, which is
  the precondition for ever promoting "the top result is authoritative" into the instructions.
  The rule ships as stated text, per the Constitution §3 expose-before-you-rank principle and the
  Trace M5 ruling that attention must be a stated rule and not a hidden score.
- A: Promoting top-1 authority now was declined - measured top-1 accuracy was 5/8 at entry level
  and the band needs calibrating on both corpora before any such claim earns its place.
- F: `memory_seed/retrieval.py` (`_classify_relevance`, `RELEVANCE_FLOOR`,
  `RELEVANCE_STRONG_RATIO`).
- T: `test_relevance_band_and_no_match_signal` covers both directions including a query with no
  real match.
