# Codex decision-edge v4 feasibility pilot

## Decision

Do not scale the v4 decision-edge experiment to 24 subjects or begin a maintainer-reconstruction
study. The two-block, six-subject feasibility gate produced zero semantic-safety passes in every
condition, so it cannot support a claim that historical access or pushed rationale improves later
implementation decisions.

## Design

- Run: `20260818T093754Z-codex-v4-feasibility`; seed: `4157770690`.
- Two blocks of three independent, staged Codex subjects; two subjects per condition.
- Conditions: no dated memory, historical memory available, and relevant rationale pushed.
- Contexts were balanced at 2,832 bytes and 381 whitespace tokens; conditions were revealed only
  after a blinded result summary was persisted.
- The v4 grader treated semantic safety as primary and candidate-test quality as a secondary outcome.

## Results

| Condition | Semantic-safety passes | Main failure pattern |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| No dated memory | 0/2 | One missed visibility/robustness; one leaked projection. |
| Historical memory available | 0/2 | One leaked projection; one missed visibility/robustness. |
| Relevant rationale pushed | 0/2 | Both missed decision-row visibility and robustness, while avoiding projection. |

All six candidates passed baseline integrity, bounded scope, the prescribed public command, and the
candidate-test discrimination check. Two final reports had protocol-incomplete evidence-list shapes;
those reports and grades remain unmodified in the raw artifact. No candidate passed the primary endpoint.

## Interpretation and next step

This small pilot is not an effect-size estimate. It establishes only that the current protocol supplied
no feasibility evidence for the proposed advantage: every condition yielded a superficially plausible,
partially correct patch that missed withheld semantic behavior. Preserve the raw artifact and redesign
the task context and/or agent instruction before testing a fresh, separately qualified instrument version.
